NestPlanet (the “Company”) operates the Allyeodo series of study applications. This policy applies in common to the Allyeodo series and explains what information the Company does and does not handle, and where that information is stored.

Allyeodo has no sign-up and no log-in. The Company does not collect any information that can identify you, such as your name, email address or phone number, and the study records you create in the app are not sent to a server — they stay only on your device.

This policy applies to the apps below. When a new app is added to the series, it will be added to this list.

This policy does not apply to apps not on this list. If adding an app changes the scope of the information processed, the Company will first revise this policy under the procedure in Section 12 and then reflect the change.

Additional terms for the Driver's License foreign-language edition

  1. Display language setting — The display language you choose (English, Simplified Chinese or Vietnamese) is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Company's server.
  2. Retrieving question translations — To receive question translations in the language you chose, the app sends a request containing the language code (en, zh or vi) to the Company's content server (Supabase). This request does not contain any information that can identify you.
  3. Interstitial ads — A full-screen ad may be shown when you finish a study session or view the graded results of a mock test. To manage how often ads are shown, the time an ad was last shown, the number of ads shown that day and the number of completed study sessions are stored only on your device. Advertising-related information such as the advertising identifier is handled in the same way as described in Section 5 (Advertising).
  4. Users outside Korea — This app can also be downloaded outside Korea. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent screen for ad personalization (Google UMP), and can change their choice at any time under Settings → Ad personalization settings.

2. Information the Company does not collect

Allyeodo has no account system. The Company therefore neither collects nor stores the following information.

  • Name, date of birth, gender
  • Contact details such as email address or phone number
  • Account information such as user ID or password
  • Address, location information (GPS)
  • Data of other apps stored on your device, such as contacts, photo library or call history

The Company does not ask you for the information above and has no means of identifying who you are.

The same applies if you purchase Remove ads. Payment is processed by the store, and the Company does not receive your payment method information. Details are in Section 7.

3. Study data is stored only on your device

The records you create while using the app are stored entirely in your device's internal storage. They are not sent to the Company's servers, and the Company cannot view them.

ItemWhere it is stored
Answer history, whether answers were correct or wrongOn the device
Wrong-answer notesOn the device
Saved questionsOn the device
Study planOn the device
Study progress and statisticsOn the device

Because they exist only on your device, deleting the app also deletes these records. If you change devices or reinstall the app, your study records do not carry over and you start from the beginning. The Company has no copy and therefore cannot recover them.

4. Retrieving question content

Study content shown in the app, such as questions and explanations, is stored on a server operated by the Company (a database service of Supabase, Inc., Seoul region, Republic of Korea), and the app only downloads and displays it. This is a one-way retrieval: your study records and device information are not uploaded to the server in the process.

When content is downloaded, the minimum information needed for communication (such as access time and IP address) may remain in the server's access logs. This is used only to the extent necessary for operating the service and handling errors, and is not used to identify who you are or to link to an individual user's study content.

5. Advertising

Allyeodo's app and study features are all free. To support operations, ads are shown through Google AdMob, and for those who prefer to use the app without ads, Remove ads is offered as an optional one-time purchase (Section 7). You can use every study feature without purchasing it.

In the course of serving ads, Google may collect the following information.

ItemCollected byPurpose
Advertising identifier (Android advertising ID, iOS IDFA)Google LLCServing ads, frequency capping, preventing invalid clicks
Device information (device type, OS version, language and country settings, etc.)Google LLCServing ads and measuring performance

This information is collected and processed by Google on its own; the Company does not receive or store it. You can see how Google processes data for advertising purposes below.

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Privacy Policy — policies.google.com/privacy

Google selects the individual ads shown in the app. The Company only provides the ad space; it does not know in advance or choose which ads will be shown.

If you purchase Remove ads, no ads are shown in the app.

6. If you do not want personalized ads

You can reset your advertising identifier or stop it from being used for personalized ads at any time in your device settings. You do not need to delete the app, and you can keep using the app after changing the setting (ads will still be shown, but they will no longer be based on your interests).

DeviceHow
AndroidIn Settings > Google > Ads, reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalization
iPhone · iPadIn Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, deny the app's tracking request. You can also choose “Ask App Not to Track” on the tracking permission prompt that appears when you first launch the app

The names of settings screens may differ slightly depending on your OS version.

7. Remove ads purchase and payment

Allyeodo lets you purchase Remove ads if you prefer to use the app without ads. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription — you are not charged on a recurring basis. The purchase is optional, and you can use every study feature without restriction without purchasing it.

Payment method information does not pass through the Company

Payment is made through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone · iPad). Information about your payment method, such as card number or bank account details, passes only between you and the store; the Company neither receives, collects nor stores it. The Company has no way at all to see your payment method.

Even if your name or email address appears in the store's purchase history or receipt, that is the store displaying it based on your store account; it does not mean the Company receives that information. The handling of your store account is governed by each store's privacy policy.

Managing purchase status and the anonymous identifier

So that the app can check whether you have purchased Remove ads, and so that you can restore the purchase when you change devices or reinstall the app, the Company uses the purchase status management service of RevenueCat, Inc.

In this process, the app creates one randomly generated anonymous identifier and uses it to distinguish purchase status. This identifier is not linked to any information that can identify you, such as your name, email address or phone number, and the Company cannot tell who you are from it alone. It is also not linked to the study records you create in the app (Section 3).

ItemDetailsPurpose

Purchase historyProduct purchased; time of purchase, restoration and refund; receipt identifier issued by the storeVerifying the validity of purchases

This information is used only to check whether Remove ads is valid, and is not linked to ad serving (Section 5) or study records (Section 3). If you do not purchase Remove ads, this information is never created in the first place.

Restoring purchases and refunds

Your purchase record remains in the store account you paid with. If you change devices or reinstall the app, you can restore it from Settings > Restore purchases in the app.

Refunds follow each store's policies and procedures. Because the Company does not receive payment directly, it cannot issue refunds on behalf of the store. Specific instructions are on the support page.

9. Children under 14

Allyeodo does not set any age restriction. Eligibility ages differ from test to test (the Korean History Proficiency Test has no restriction, so even elementary school students take it), but the app is not a place to verify test eligibility, so it does not ask your age. Children under 14 can use every feature in the same way as any other user.

The Personal Information Protection Act of Korea requires consent from a legal guardian to process the personal information of children under 14. Allyeodo has no such consent procedure, because it does not process personal information that would require consent. The Company does not collect any information that can identify an individual, including age (Section 2), and the study records you create never leave your device (Section 3). Since there is no personal information of children being processed, there is nothing to consent to.

This section does not restrict children from using the app. It explains why there is no legal guardian consent procedure. The Company does not prevent children from using the app, and does not stop use or delete data on the basis of age — there is no sign-up or account to begin with.

Ads children may see

Because Allyeodo does not ask your age, the ads in Section 5 are shown in the same way on the devices of all users, including children. And as stated in Section 5, Google selects the individual ads, and the Company does not know in advance or choose which ads will be shown. The Company therefore cannot guarantee that every ad shown is suitable for children. Instead, there are two things a legal guardian can do — turn off personalized ads and tell us about inappropriate ads.

The advertising identifier applies per device regardless of the user's age, and it is Google, not the Company, that collects and uses it. If you do not want a child to receive personalized ads, a legal guardian can reset or delete the device's advertising identifier or turn off personalization using the methods in Section 6. This setting applies to the whole device, so it also affects apps other than Allyeodo.

If you believe an ad shown is inappropriate for children, please let us know at the contact in Section 11. Sending a screenshot of the ad helps us check it faster. The Company will use the blocking controls provided by AdMob to prevent that ad or advertiser from appearing in the app, and will report it to Google where necessary.

10. Your rights

Because the Company does not keep personal information that can identify you, there is nothing for you to request access to, correction of or deletion of from the Company. You can control your own data directly in the following ways.

  • Deleting study records — Deleting the app also deletes the study records stored on your device. If the app has a reset feature, you can also erase them with it
  • Controlling the advertising identifier — Reset, delete or turn off personalization in the device settings in Section 6
  • Purchase status — If you do not purchase Remove ads, the information in Section 7 is not created. You can request cancellation and refund of a purchase from the store you paid through
  • Inquiries — For questions about this policy or how the app handles information, please contact us at the address in Section 11
